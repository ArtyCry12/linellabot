FAQ

Do you store our source code?

No. FlowMetrics reads metadata via GitHub/GitLab APIs — commits, PRs, deploy events. Enterprise can run entirely in your VPC.

How fast is setup?

Most teams connect GitHub and see a live dashboard in under 90 seconds. Historical backfill runs in the background.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Team plans are month-to-month. Export your data before you leave — we don't hold it hostage.
