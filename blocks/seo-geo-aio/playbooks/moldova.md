# Moldova-first research playbook

## Local signals

- Official / legal: legis.md, gov.md, ANPC/consumer where relevant
- News: local MD outlets + regional RO/UA when topic crosses border
- Maps/GMB: NAP consistency, categories, reviews, photos, posts
- Language: Romanian (MD) primary; Russian when audience requires

## GEO / AIO

- Prefer clear entity pages (org, service, city)
- Cite local facts with dates
- Avoid generic EU fluff as if it were MD law

## Sources to mix

Google · GitHub (local OSS) · Reddit (r/moldova, niche) · Telegram public channels · news RSS

## Output

Brief in project `seo/research/md-<topic>.md` with: queries, entities, competitors, GMB gaps, content angles.
