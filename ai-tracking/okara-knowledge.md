## Patterns to adopt (not copy product)

1. **Human-in-the-loop by default** — drafts for approval, not auto-post (matches Zapier safety rule).
2. **Daily/weekly action queue** — surface 3–5 next actions, not infinite task lists.
3. **Cross-channel from one brief** — one product update → Reddit + X + blog angle.
4. **GEO as first-class** — separate from classic SEO in routing (`auto-orchestrator`).
5. **UGC agent = brief → aspects → download** — mirror in production-studio segment table.
6. **Integrations as sensors** — GSC/GA inform priority, not just reporting.
